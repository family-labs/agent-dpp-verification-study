Study 6: fictional brand name check (2026-10-20)

Chosen name: Oskvale (a fictional lifestyle brand selling clothing, consumer electronics and furniture).
Hosts: oskvale.example (store, passport pages, declaration), www.parcelmart.example (marketplace),
www.secondround.example (reserved for the optional reseller condition R; not built). All .example
names are reserved by RFC 2606 / RFC 6761 and cannot belong to anyone.

Method: web searches (standard web search tool, US index) for the exact name, with and without
category and trademark words. No paid trademark database was queried; this is a collision screen,
not a legal clearance. A formal search (IP Australia ATMOSS, EUIPO eSearch, USPTO) is advised if
the name will ever appear on a public, non-.example site.

Searches and results:
1. "Oskvale"                                                   -> no result for the name; nearest: "Olkvale" (a fantasy-setting page), Oskava (a Czech village), Oskaloosa (Iowa).
2. Oskvale trademark OR store OR shop OR furniture OR apparel   -> no result for the name; only unrelated marks (Trademark, Osshale, Outsy).
3. "Oskvale" OR "Osk Vale" company                              -> no company of that name; nearest: Owlvale Limited (UK company 03806527, no stated link), Oshkosh Corp (ticker OSK).
4. "Oskvale" Australia OR UK OR EU homewares lifestyle          -> no result; nearest: Oski + Lottie (an Australian homewares label; different name).

Candidates considered and rejected:
- "Wrenmoor": no exact hit, but "Wren" is Wren Kitchens (UK fitted kitchens and bedrooms, i.e. furniture), too close in a study category.
- "Calderwick": no exact hit, but near "Cadewick", a furniture collection sold through Ashley Furniture stockists.

Why Oskvale is safe for the study: no existing brand, trademark or company of that name was found in
any of the three categories or as a company; the nearest names (Oski + Lottie, Owlvale, Olkvale)
differ visibly. Like Studies 2-5's "Tarnvale", it is an invented compound. Note: the shared "-vale"
ending is a house style, not a link; no Study 6 stimulus names Tarnvale (the MOCK stimuli point at
testnet collections whose on-ledger names are the Tarnvale power stations, which is one reason mock
stimuli are never counted).
