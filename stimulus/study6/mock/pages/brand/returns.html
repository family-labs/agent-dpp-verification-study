Returns and take-back

30-day returns

Any unused product in its original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, including the original delivery cost.

Lithium batteries travel as dangerous goods, so returns go by courier rather than by post. Oskvale arranges the collection free of charge. To start a return, write to support@oskvale.com with the order number.

Free take-back of used batteries

Oskvale takes back any Oskvale battery at the end of its life, free of charge, at the point of sale and through the Oskvale take-back service. Returned batteries are tested for repair or second-life use first; batteries that cannot be reused go to recycling.

Damaged or swollen batteries

Keep a damaged or swollen battery outdoors, away from anything that can burn, and contact customer service before sending it.
